# Veil Nebula background

“Return to the Veil Nebula”, Hubble Space Telescope observation, released 29 March 2021.

**Credit: ESA/Hubble & NASA, Z. Levay**

The 4000 × 2802 publication JPEG was resized to 2560 and 1280 pixels wide
and encoded as WebP, quality 85. No generated or painted detail was added.
The site crops responsively and dims the image in its background shader,
with camera parallax and a light echo during planet captures. These are
presentation effects by tomgreen.ai, not part of the telescope observation.
